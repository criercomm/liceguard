The 14-day plan that breaks the lice life cycle

Lice eggs hatch over about a week, and new lice start laying their own eggs about a week after that. Two weeks of daily combing catches every wave.

Does my child have lice?

Itching is the classic sign, but on a first case it can take four to six weeks to start, and many kids never itch at all. A careful look is the only way to know.

Where to look

Behind the ears and at the nape of the neck, close to the scalp. Lice are about the size of a sesame seed and move fast, away from light.

Eggs or dandruff?

Dandruff brushes off. Eggs are glued to one side of the hair and won't budge when you flick them. Eggs within a quarter inch of the scalp are the ones most likely to be live.

What confirms it

A live, crawling louse. Old nits far down the hair shaft are usually already hatched or dead and don't mean an active case on their own.

How to prevent lice at school, camp and sleepovers

Lice crawl. They can't jump or fly, so nearly every case comes from heads touching.

  • Tie long hair back in a braid or bun
  • Don't share hats, helmets, brushes, hair ties or headphones
  • Spray LiceGuard Repellent each morning during busy seasons
  • Screen weekly with the RobiComb, and more often after an exposure

What to clean, and what to skip

A louse off the head dies within about two days, so focus on what touched the head in the last 48 hours.

  • Wash bedding, hats and towels in hot water (130°F) and dry on high heat
  • Soak combs and brushes in 130°F water for 5 to 10 minutes
  • Seal stuffed animals and anything unwashable in a bag for two weeks
  • Vacuum the couch, car seats and carpet
  • Skip lice foggers and household sprays. The CDC says they aren't needed and can be toxic.

Questions parents ask

What is the best non-toxic lice treatment for kids that works on super lice?

Choose a method that doesn't rely on pesticides like permethrin or pyrethrins, because super lice resist them. Physical removal works regardless of resistance. LiceGuard pairs the RobiComb, which kills live lice on contact in dry hair, with the No-Nit Kit, which loosens and combs out eggs and nits, repeated over 14 days.

Do electric lice combs work?

An electric comb like the RobiComb kills the live lice it touches in dry hair and helps you find them, because the hum pauses on contact. It doesn't reliably remove eggs glued close to the scalp, so pair it with a fine metal nit comb and use it daily for two weeks to catch lice as they hatch.

What are super lice, and how are they different from regular lice?

Super lice are ordinary head lice that have become resistant to common over-the-counter treatments containing permethrin or pyrethrins. They look the same, spread the same way and cause the same itching. They're just harder to kill with those products, so non-chemical methods often work better.

Do lice repellent sprays work?

A repellent lowers the odds; it doesn't replace checks. LiceGuard Lice Repellent Hair Spray is clinically proven to repel lice for up to 24 hours. Apply it each morning as the last product in the hair, keep long hair tied back, avoid sharing hats and brushes, and check heads weekly.

How long does it take to get rid of lice?

Plan on 14 days. According to the CDC, eggs hatch in about 6 to 9 days, and young lice take about 7 more days to mature and lay eggs. Daily combing for two weeks catches every new hatch before it can lay eggs.

How do I tell lice eggs from dandruff?

Dandruff flakes slide or brush off easily. Lice eggs are glued to one side of the hair shaft and don't move when you flick them. Eggs within about a quarter inch of the scalp are the ones most likely to be active.

Do I need to clean my whole house after lice?

No. Lice off the head die within about two days. Wash bedding, hats and towels used in the last two days in hot water and dry on high heat, soak combs and brushes in hot water, seal anything unwashable in a bag for two weeks, and vacuum. The CDC says fumigant sprays and fogs aren't needed and can be toxic.
